Experiment explorer 2

Every stored run, on OVO-Bench val and ProactiveVideoQA EGO. Nothing is re-run: each model's p(Yes) per 2 s tick is embedded, and the threshold, response rule and scores are computed here. Threshold: each model fires on the chosen share of its own moments (models say Yes at very different base rates). Rules: crossing = respond when p(Yes) rises above the threshold; re-arm = also respond again after the log-odds fall 1.0 below the stretch's peak and recover 1.0; every 6 s / 10 s = after the crossing, respond again every 6 or 10 s while still above. One-answer questions (clue reveal) always answer once, the first time. Scoring: OVO, an event is caught by a response within 2 s; EGO, a reply interval is caught by a response inside it; a false alarm is a response that catches nothing. Repeats on one event are not punished. The timer makes the same number of responses per question, evenly spaced. No confidence intervals here; see docs/RATIONALE.md and docs/ARCHITECTURE_REPORT.md.
